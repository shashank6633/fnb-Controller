# Valet — shift guide (for the Valet Head and attendants)

Plain steps for a normal night. Screens: **Valet Head Dashboard** (`/valet`), **Attendant**
(`/valet/attendant`, phone), **Desk** (`/valet/desk`, host/cashier), **Settings**, **Reports**.

## Before opening (Valet Head)

1. Valet Settings: valet is **ON** for this outlet; zones and key tags are there; staff on tonight
   are ticked under "Staff for this outlet" (owner/admin does this once).
2. The venue QR standee / NFC card is out at the drop-off point.
3. Read last night's **shift handover note** (Head Dashboard → Team).

## Attendants — start of shift

* Open **Attendant** on your phone → **Start shift**. You cannot check cars in until you do.
* Keep the page open: the board uses it to know you are reachable ("device quiet" shows when not).

## A car arrives

* **Guest has the pass on their phone** (they scanned the QR): **New check-in → Guest has a code**,
  type their 6-digit code, check the car matches, put a key tag on the keys, **Check in**.
* **Guest has no phone / no time**: **New check-in → Staff check-in**, fill plate + tag, **Check in**,
  then **No phone — print a claim receipt** (the receipt's code is what gets the car back).
* **Paper-ticket outlet**: type the paper ticket number (and a key tag if you use both).
* Photos: **Condition photos** — front, rear, sides, any damage. Skip only with a reason.
* Park it: **Set parking spot** → zone + bay + where the keys go (rack/hook, or with you).

## A guest asks for the car

* Requests arrive from the guest's pass, the desk/cashier ("Guest asked" ticked), or WhatsApp NOW.
* Attendants press **Take next car** — the app hands out the oldest due request, one at a time; two
  people can never take the same car.
* **Start fetching** → bring it to the gate → **Mark ready at gate**. The guest's pass shows "ready" and a
  6-digit code that changes every 90 seconds.
* **Handover**: the guest shows the code on their phone (or the claim receipt). Type it, give the keys.
  Press **Verify handover**, type the tag on the keys in your hand and the code, then **Hand over the car**.
  Never hand over on a tag number, plate or name alone.

## Guest not there yet

* The gate timer (2/3/5/10 min, set by the owner) runs. If it ends: **Guest went back inside** or
  **Move to holding** (the car goes to the holding bay; the guest's pass says so).
* When they come back: **Guest has returned** → bring it round → handover as usual.

## If something goes wrong

* **Guest lost their phone and receipt**: the Valet Head uses **Verify handover / recovery** in the
  car's drawer — records how the guest was verified (vehicle papers shown, bill or reservation
  matched, a manager knows the guest, ownership questions answered) and what happened. Logged. Do not
  photograph ID cards.
* **Wrong car / damage / complaint**: **Record incident** in the drawer. An incident can keep the
  car's record and photos past normal deletion ("incident hold").
* **Board shows "Needs attention"**: unclaimed requests, long fetches, cars waiting at the gate,
  cars not parked after 15 min, failed WhatsApp messages. Press **Seen** when you have it in hand.
* **Internet down**: the attendant phone keeps the check-in draft for 30 minutes. Write tag + plate on
  paper and enter them when it is back. Never release a car without the guest's code / receipt /
  head recovery.

## Paper-ticket fallback

Paper books must be registered first (Settings → Paper ticket books). A ticket number alone never
releases a car: the guest who types a ticket at the table gets a code, and **staff must look at the
paper stub** and tick "stub checked" before the pass appears.

## End of shift

* Hand over every key and car you hold — **End shift** is refused while you still hold keys.
* Valet Head: **Write shift handover** (open cars, keys, issues are added automatically).

## Privacy and clean-up

* Guest names/numbers are masked on screens; **Show number** asks for a reason and is logged.
* After the outlet's retention period (default 12 months, set by the owner) the daily clean-up
  removes plate, guest name/number and photos from old records — except cars on incident hold.
  This is a setting, not automatic legal compliance.

## NFC and RFID (optional)

* **Key-tag NFC sticker**: tapping it with a staff phone opens that car (staff sign-in needed).
  A copied sticker read by a different chip raises an alert — the head gives the tag a **New link**.
* **RFID** readers (if fitted later) only *hint* where keys were seen. They never move or release a car.
